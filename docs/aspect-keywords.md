## Aspect Types

| Aspect | Angle | Keyword |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **Conjunction** | 0° | Merge and amplify. The two planets operate as one force, their energies fused and inseparable. What one does, the other does with it. The conjunction is the most powerful aspect because it removes the distance between two principles. They act together, for better or worse. |
| **Opposition** | 180° | Polarity and tension. Two forces face each other across the chart, each pulling in opposite directions. The opposition creates awareness through contrast. You cannot see one side without the other. The work is integration: holding both truths at once rather than choosing between them. |
| **Trine** | 120° | Flow and ease. The planets support each other without friction, their energies moving in the same direction. Talent that comes without effort, harmony that can be taken for granted. The trine is a gift, but gifts don't demand growth. The risk is inertia. The thing that comes easily may never be fully developed. |
| **Square** | 90° | Friction and drive. Two forces at cross-purposes, each blocking the other's natural expression. The square is the engine of growth through resistance. It creates tension that demands resolution. The planets don't get along, and that's the point. The friction produces heat, and heat produces change. |
| **Sextile** | 60° | Opportunity and invitation. The planets are in compatible elements, their energies different but cooperative. The sextile doesn't force anything. It offers a door. You have to open it. It's the aspect of potential that requires action to activate, easier than a square but less automatic than a trine. |

## Aspect Patterns

| Pattern | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **T-Square** | Dynamic tension between planets in mutual square. Two planets oppose each other, and a third squares both, creating a pressure cooker that demands resolution. The T-Square produces relentless drive because the tension never fully releases. It is the pattern of people who achieve through friction. The focal planet, the one receiving both squares, is where the pressure concentrates and where the breakthrough happens. |
| **Grand Trine** | A closed loop of flowing trines. Three planets, each 120 degrees from the next, forming a triangle of effortless harmony. Talent comes without struggle, and things work without apparent effort. The Grand Trine is a gift, but gifts do not demand growth. The risk is inertia: the person who never develops their talent because they never had to. The work is to activate what comes easily rather than coasting on it. |
| **Yod (Finger of God)** | The Finger of God. Two planets in sextile both form quincunx aspects to a third, focal planet, creating a narrow triangle that points to a single point of pressure. The quincunx is an aspect of adjustment: the two base planets support each other, but neither understands the focal planet. The focal planet receives energy it cannot integrate, creating a fated, uncomfortable pressure that feels like a mission you did not choose. The Yod is the pattern of those who are driven by something they cannot explain. |
| **Grand Cross** | Four planets in mutual squares, forming a closed box of tension. Each planet squares two others and opposes a third. There is no escape and no release. The Grand Cross produces extraordinary resilience because the native learns to function under constant pressure. It is the pattern of those who can handle what breaks others. The work is not to resolve the tension but to channel it productively. |
| **Cradle** | A bowl of support. Three planets connected by sextiles and trines, with an opposition across the open end. The sextiles and trines form a nurturing structure that holds and protects. The opposition provides the productive tension that keeps the cradle from becoming stagnant. It is the pattern of those who are supported in ways they may not recognize until the support is withdrawn. The work is to use the safety of the cradle to take risks at the open end. |
| **Kite** | A Grand Trine with an opposition. The trine triangle provides effortless flow, and the opposition provides a release valve and a direction. The planet at the tip of the Kite, opposite the base of the Grand Trine, is where the energy discharges. It is the pattern of talent that finds an outlet. The Kite is more dynamic than a Grand Trine alone because the opposition forces engagement with the world. The work is to aim the release rather than letting it fire randomly. |
| **Stellium** | A concentration of three or more planets in one sign or house. The energies of the stellium planets fuse into a single, intensified focus. The sign or house containing the stellium becomes the dominant force in the chart, overwhelming other placements. It is the pattern of those who are specialists rather than generalists. The gift is depth and intensity in one area of life. The cost is that other areas may be neglected or underdeveloped. |

## How the Engine Combines Them

`InterpretAspect()` produces output like:

> **Sun conjunction Moon (2.3°):** Conscious will meets emotional need. The fundamental axis of personality... *[pair dynamic]* Merge and amplify. The two planets operate as one force... *[aspect keyword]*

The pair dynamic comes first, then the aspect keyword. The engine looks up pairs order-independently — `Sun,Moon` and `Moon,Sun` both resolve to the same text.
